# The Daily Sum — Saturday 21 November 2026

One page, one day: **35 stories** from Saturday 21 November 2026, drawn as a
cream broadsheet whose masthead is the date itself. A colour-coded region bar
(Europe 4 · Asia 10 · Middle East 5 · Africa 1 · Americas 11 · Oceania 3 ·
Global 1) and a six-number stats strip sit under the red three-story subhead.

The three leads:

1. **Antalya** — COP31 runs into overtime, no deal on day 13.
2. **Riyadh** — three ballistic missiles downed over the capital before dawn.
3. **Junín, Peru** — a magnitude 6.7 quake, felt in Lima, harms no one.

Ten more stories get drawn tiles with verification pills (stories 4–13), the
remaining 22 run as a numbered briefs list beside a dotted world map with a
pin for every drawn story, and a DATE CHECK footer names the tempting stories
left out as other days' news — and the two that had not happened yet.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```

Writes `entry.png` (1024×1024) to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR`, or `/output` when the
variable is unset. Python 3.13 with Pillow; no network; two runs produce
byte-identical output. The page is rendered at 2048×2048, downscaled for crisp
text, and palette-quantized to keep the file small.
